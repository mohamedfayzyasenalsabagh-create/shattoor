Samir and the Brids
         A beautiful, bright green pea-
         pod is growing in the sunshine in
         a field full of pea-vines.




         The pod is full of juicy, fat, bright
         green peas that are nearly ready
         to be picked.




          A hungry caterpillar has found
          its way into the pod and is
          eating the juicy bright green
          peas inside.




        A bird sees the caterpillar, flies
        down and swallows it whole.
